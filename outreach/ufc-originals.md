# UFC / TKO — cold outreach

## Who to write to

**Not** HR, and not through Workday. Applications there die on the work-authorization question.

Write to **producers in UFC Originals Production** (Las Vegas). Their own job descriptions state
that producers "manage freelance crews" and communicate with "production vendors" — buying outside
hands is literally part of their job. They can hire a contractor without involving immigration.

Search LinkedIn for, at UFC / Ultimate Fighting Championship:
- Producer, Originals Production
- Senior Producer / Supervising Producer
- Director, Content Production
- Social Media Manager / Director, Digital

Secondary: TKO Group Holdings staff with "content" or "production" in the title.

## Why contractor, not employee

Zuffa LLC has filed only 6 H-1B applications since 2021, all at Supervising Producer / Senior
Producer / Director level ($105K–$191K). Coordinator-tier roles are never sponsored. A remote
contract sidesteps the entire question.

## Notes on sending

- Attach or link **specific viral videos**, not the profile page — the recent feed is cold and
  undercuts the pitch.
- Keep it under 200 words. Producers skim.
- One follow-up after 8–10 days, then stop.
