# Migu (咪咕) — cold outreach

## Why Migu, and why now

- **Feb 2021** — Migu becomes UFC's exclusive full-media partner in mainland China: mobile, PC, TV,
  VR; exclusive 5G/4K/8K signal production; 12+ numbered events and 30 Fight Nights a year; joint
  production of original films, documentaries and reality content.
- **4 March 2026** — UFC extends the partnership **to 2031**, and upgrades Migu to **exclusive
  co-organizer** of UFC events in mainland China. That is a jump from distributing to producing.
- **28–29 August 2026** — the upgrade lands: Migu co-organizes UFC Fight Night Shanghai alongside
  Orange Lion Sports. 16,188 attendance — an Asian Fight Night record. Migu ships 3D multi-angle
  4K with AI scene generation, "bullet time" strike breakdowns, and exclusive backstage content
  from 26 August: presser, weigh-in, open workouts, and the "中国丽亮的UFC记忆" feature on Zhang
  Weili and Li Jingliang.

Translation: their content volume just multiplied, and their production appetite with it.

## Structure

China Mobile → 咪咕文化科技 (Migu Culture, Beijing) → **咪咕视讯科技 (Migu Video, Shanghai)**.
