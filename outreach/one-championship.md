# ONE Championship — cold outreach

## Why direct contact is the only route

ONE's careers portal (careers.onefc.com) is down — it returns a server error. Their openings live
on LinkedIn only. Writing to producers directly is not bypassing a process; it *is* the process.

Roles seen open recently: Freelance Associate Producer (Manila), Freelance Assistant Production
Manager, Freelance Creative Designer, Associate Producer, Executive Social Media. **ONE hires
freelancers as standard practice** — that's the opening.

## Who to write to

On LinkedIn, at "ONE" / ONE Championship:
- Senior Content Producer & Editor (Singapore)
- Associate Producer / Producer (Singapore, Manila)
- Head of Content / Creative Director

Company context: Group ONE Holdings, HQ Singapore, ~221 staff across 34 countries, broadcast to
190+ countries. Offices include Shanghai and Bangkok.

## Notes

- ONE's Muay Thai and kickboxing verticals are worth naming if you have relevant footage — it
  signals you actually watch the product rather than just MMA.
