### What separates the top tier from the bottom

Recurring requirements in 20–30K listings:

1. **English as a working language** — "英语可作为工作语言", "拒绝中式英语", English copy tests at interview
2. **出海 focus** — TikTok / Instagram / X / YouTube, and transcreation rather than translation
3. **Ownership of the whole chain** — 编导 conceives and is accountable; 剪辑师 executes. That's the pay gap.
4. **An AI workflow** — "深度使用AI工具，并有自己AI工作流"; ability to catch AI's factual and cultural errors

Strategic note: the 20–30K market in Shanghai is **出海 brands** (Anker, TYMO, MOVA, Shokz),
not sports media. Easier entry for a foreigner, better pay, and it builds the local track record
and visa history that a later move into sports content requires.
